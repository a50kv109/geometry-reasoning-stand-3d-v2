# LVG-001: Local Vertex Geometry Specification

## 1. Mathematical Formulation

For each vertex $V \in \{A, B, C, D\}$ of the tetrahedron inscribed in $S^2(O, R)$, let its 3 incident edge vectors be:
$$\mathbf{e}_i = \mathbf{v}_i - \mathbf{v}_V, \quad i \in \{1, 2, 3\}$$

Normalized unit directions:
$$\mathbf{u}_i = \frac{\mathbf{e}_i}{\|\mathbf{e}_i\|}$$

### Gram Matrix
$$G_{ij} = \mathbf{u}_i \cdot \mathbf{u}_j, \quad G \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times 3}$$

### Solid Angle ($\Omega_V$)
Using the Oosterom-Strackee spherical excess formula with continuous two-argument arctangent:
$$\tan\left(\frac{\Omega_V}{2}\right) = \frac{|\mathbf{u}_1 \cdot (\mathbf{u}_2 \times \mathbf{u}_3)|}{1 + \mathbf{u}_1 \cdot \mathbf{u}_2 + \mathbf{u}_2 \cdot \mathbf{u}_3 + \mathbf{u}_3 \cdot \mathbf{u}_1}$$
$$\Omega_V = 2 \operatorname{atan2}(|\mathbf{u}_1 \cdot (\mathbf{u}_2 \times \mathbf{u}_3)|, D)$$

## 2. Invariants
- **Scale Invariance:** $\Omega_V(\lambda R) = \Omega_V(R)$, $\alpha_{ij}(\lambda R) = \alpha_{ij}(R)$.
- **Translation Invariance:** $\Omega_V(S + \mathbf{t}) = \Omega_V(S)$.
- **Rotation Invariance:** $\operatorname{Tr}(G)$, $\det(G)$, and $\Omega_V$ remain invariant under $S \mathcal{R}$.
